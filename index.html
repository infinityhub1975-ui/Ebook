Three years ago, she shot him.

Nora Quinn is Halcyon's coldest operative. Cole Mercer is Meridian's most charming liar. In Istanbul, she put a bullet in his shoulder. He kept it.

Now their rival agencies need one impossible thing: a married couple. At a dying broker's villa on Lake Como, they must pose as Mr. and Mrs. Hale and steal the Lantern List, the names of sleeper agents who will die if it ever goes on sale.

The cover story is easy. The rules are easier: don't touch, don't improvise, don't fall in love. But the villa has no locks on its doors, the host can read a lie in a husband's glance, and there is only one bed.

And on the last morning, both of them get the same order: .

Slow burn, banter, a very large dog, and a happy ending. Kisses and one tasteful fade-to-black scene; nothing explicit.

What you're signing up for

Fake marriage

Six years of invented history, a wedding story they have to tell in front of twelve strangers, and a ring that fits far too well.

Forced proximity

One suite. One enormous bed. No locks on any door. A fortress of pillows that does not survive the week.

Enemies to lovers

They know exactly how the other fights. They are about to learn how the other takes their coffee, and what they're afraid of at three in the morning.

Danger

A vault under the library, a staged ambush on the lake, a traitor with a very good watch, and orders neither of them can live with.
